I'm excited to share that I am joining the **Actuaries Institute's YAP Career & Leadership Working Group** as a founding member.

This volunteer role formalises the work I have been contributing to through the Institute's Career Series: helping support events, shape themes, and create spaces where early-career actuaries can learn from one another and from more experienced members of the profession.

## Why This Matters to Me

I care a lot about technical excellence, but I also care about the professional communities that help people grow into that excellence. The actuarial pathway can be demanding, and I know how valuable it is to have access to practical career conversations, generous mentors, and peers who are navigating similar questions.

Through this working group, I will be contributing to:

- Supporting the Actuaries Institute Career Series
- Helping develop themes that are relevant to young actuaries and early-career professionals
- Contributing ideas for the Young Actuaries Conference
- Building stronger links between technical development, leadership, and community engagement

For me, this is a natural extension of the work I already enjoy: mentoring, event support, professional communication, and helping make actuarial spaces feel more accessible.

## Building the Profession I Want to Be Part Of

One of the things I value most about the Actuaries Institute is that it gives members opportunities to contribute beyond their day-to-day technical roles. Volunteering with the Institute is a chance to help shape the conversations that younger members are having about careers, leadership, and the future of actuarial work.

I'm grateful to be involved from the beginning of this working group, and I am looking forward to contributing to initiatives that help young actuaries feel more connected, more confident, and more ambitious about the profession.
